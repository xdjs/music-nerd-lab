What powers this version
• Built with v0; Next.js 16, React 19 and TypeScript.
• Gemini 2.5 Flash through @google/generative-ai and a server-side /api/analyze route. GEMINI_API_KEY stays on the server.
• Browser camera, canvas capture and FileReader upload. Images are sent to the server and Google Gemini for analysis.
• Two analysis requests: three initial facts, then four more requested when you reach the second fact. This is progressive loading, not token streaming.
• Tailwind CSS, Radix-based UI components, Lucide icons and Framer Motion. Anton headings, Inter body text, Music Nerd branding.
• Browser localStorage for up to 50 saved scans, with delete and clear-history controls. Native Web Share where supported.
• Vercel hosting and Vercel Analytics.

How it evolved
The build history records prompt fixes for guessed identities, broader music connections for everyday objects, clickable source links, shorter names/headlines, long-text and overflow fixes, and a move from stacked cards to a full-screen horizontal fact sequence with simpler overlays and direction-aware transitions.

Current limits
Facts and source links are generated by the model. The inspected route has no web-search tool or independent source verification. Saved history keeps the image and initial result locally; the extra deep-pass facts are not merged into that saved result. It is not synced to your Music Nerd account. The app does not currently read or write Music Nerd API/database records. The recipe is an adaptable starter prompt, not the original conversation or a claim that every suggested check already passes.
